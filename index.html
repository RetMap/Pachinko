How to Play

  • Start with 250 balls. Land them in scoring buckets to earn points and earn more balls.
  • POWER oscillates left ↔ right automatically. The marker shows your current power.
  • HOLD the FIRE button 🔴 to launch balls from the spout at the top. Power = launch speed = where the ball arcs.
  • Aim for buckets — easy ones near the spout give few points & 1 ball; corner pockets are hardest but pay +1500 pts & +15 balls.
  • 🌷 Tulips open & close. Hit one open for +800 pts & +10 balls.
  • 🎰 The CENTER JACKPOT spins the slot machine — match symbols for huge bonuses & FEVER mode.
  • Hit consecutive scoring objects for a combo multiplier up to x5.
  • ✨ Gold balls spawn randomly — they're worth 2x points.
  • Balls that fall to the bottom drain are lost. Game ends when you run out.
